Gears I use

Iphone 15 PRO

iPhone 15 Pro delivers flagship-level images with a 48MP main camera, improved low-light performance, and Smart HDR for natural skin tones and balanced highlights. The Pro camera system adds telephoto reach for portraits and detail shots, plus an ultra-wide lens for landscapes and tight spaces. It also supports ProRAW and Cinematic-style video for more control in editing and smoother storytelling.

Adcom AD-25MM Professional HD 10x Macro Lens

Designed for extreme close-ups to capture detail and texture. The hood helps keep consistent focus distance and softens light for more even results—great for insects, food, plants, jewelry, and more.
